# 公开活动接口

公开活动相关接口，带频率限制（每分钟60次）。

---

## POST /api/public/activity/register - 活动用户注册

---

## POST /api/public/activity/login - 活动用户登录

---

## GET /api/public/activities - 公开活动列表

---

## GET /api/public/activities/:id - 活动详情

---

## GET /api/public/activities/:id/joined - 检查是否已参加

---

## GET /api/public/activities/:id/winners - 获取中奖名单

---

## GET /api/public/activities/:id/my-cards - 获取我领取的卡号

---

## POST /api/public/activities/:id/spin - 转盘抽奖

---

## POST /api/public/activities/:id/join - 参加定时开奖

---

## POST /api/public/activities/:id/claim - 领取卡号

---

## GET /api/public/activities/my-records - 我的活动记录

---

## POST /api/public/withdraw - 公开提卡

不限频率，用于客户端批量提卡场景。
