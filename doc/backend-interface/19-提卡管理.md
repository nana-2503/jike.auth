# 提卡管理接口

需要登录。管理提卡Key和提卡日志。

---

## GET /api/user/withdraw-keys - 提卡Key列表

**Query参数:** page, size

---

## POST /api/user/withdraw-keys - 创建提卡Key

---

## PUT /api/user/withdraw-keys/:id - 更新提卡Key

---

## DELETE /api/user/withdraw-keys/:id - 删除提卡Key

---

## POST /api/user/withdraw-keys/:id/reset - 重置提卡Key

---

## GET /api/user/withdraw-logs - 提卡日志

**Query参数:** page, size

---

## 公开提卡接口

### POST /api/public/withdraw - 提卡

公开接口，不限频率，用于客户端批量提卡场景。
